You'vebeencursed

The thing you were just handed is cursed. Get rid of it.

Nobody else knows this game exists. Keep it that way.

The rules

  • The hosts secretly check who has it at random times all night. Each time it's you, your name goes on their list.
  • It has to stay with you. No putting it down, hiding it, or leaving it on a table. Get caught and your name goes on the list.
  • The only way out: someone takes it from your hand willingly. No tossing it, no forcing it.
  • Tell no one, except the person you pass it to.

Passing it on

  1. Get someone to take it from your hand.
  2. Once it's in their hand, lean in and whisper:

    “That's cursed. Get rid of it. Don't tell anyone.”

  3. Send them this page.
  4. You're free. But if someone tricks you into taking it back, it's yours again.

End of the night

One big reveal. The hosts read out who had it at every check. Everyone on the list pulls a punishment from the jar and performs. The most-caught gets the worst one.

Tips

  • Ask for help: “Can you hold this while I grab a plate?”
  • Hand it over with a drink or a sweet.
  • Pick people who look busy or deep in conversation.
  • Act normal. Never look guilty.
  • Be suspicious when someone hands you something.
Now smile, and hand it over.
