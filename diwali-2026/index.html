Mafia

A few of you are secretly the Mafia. Every night they remove one player. Every day the town votes out who they think the Mafia is.

Your role

You'll get a secret role. Don't show it to anyone.

How a round goes

Night

Everyone closes their eyes.

  1. Mafia open their eyes, point at who to remove, then close.
  2. The Doctor opens, points at who to save, then closes.
  3. The Detective opens and points at a suspect. The narrator nods yes or shakes no.
  4. Everyone opens their eyes.

Day

  1. The narrator tells the story of who was removed. If the Doctor saved them, nobody is out.
  2. Discuss and accuse for about 5 minutes.
  3. Vote. Most votes is out and reveals their role.
  4. Back to night.

Once you're out, stay silent. No hints, no faces.

How to win

  • Town wins when every Mafia member is out.
  • Mafia wins when they equal or outnumber everyone else.

Table rules

  • Eyes closed means closed.
  • No swearing on anything to prove you're innocent.
  • Never show your role, even when you're out.
  • No tapping, coughing, or giggling at night.
  • The narrator's word is final.

Two Rooms and a Boom

Two teams, two rooms, three rounds. Your number picks your team. If the Bomber ends up in the same room as the King, Red wins. If not, Blue wins.

Your number

The host gives everyone a chit with a number. Keep it hidden.

Setup

  • Everyone is split randomly into two rooms: the living room and the den.
  • Each room picks a leader by pointing. Most of the room can point at someone new to replace them anytime.

Three rounds, each shorter

3 minthen send 2
2 minthen send 1
1 minthen send 1
  1. Talk and share. Pair up with anyone and privately show either just your number (your team) or your whole chit (your role). Say anything out loud, but a chit can't lie. You can refuse.
  2. Pick hostages. When time's up, each leader picks who to send to the other room. Leaders can't send themselves.
  3. Swap. Hostages walk across at the same time. No talking between rooms.

How to win

After the last swap, everyone shows their chit.

  • Red wins if the Bomber and the King share a room.
  • Blue wins if they're in different rooms.

Tips

  • King: show only your number unless you're sure.
  • Bomber: act like a helpful blue. Ask others to show first.
  • The leader picks hostages, so fight for the job if you have a plan.
  • Watch who refuses to share.
