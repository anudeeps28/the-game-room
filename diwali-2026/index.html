Mafia

A few of you are secretly the Mafia. Each night, they kill one person. Each day, everyone votes out the person they think is Mafia.

How a round goes

Night

Everyone closes their eyes. The host calls each group in turn.

  1. Mafia open their eyes, point at who to kill, and close them.
  2. The Doctor opens, points at who to save, and closes.
  3. The Detective opens and points at someone. The host nods for Mafia, shakes for not.
  4. Everyone opens their eyes.

Day

  1. The host says who was killed. If the Doctor saved them, nobody dies.
  2. Talk for about 5 minutes. Who's lying?
  3. Vote. The person with the most votes is out and shows their card.
  4. Night again.

Once you're out, stay quiet. No hints, no faces.

How to win

  • Villagers win when all the Mafia are out.
  • Mafia win when there are as many Mafia left as everyone else.

House rules

  • Closed eyes stay closed. No peeking.
  • No swearing on God or your mother to prove you're innocent.
  • Never show your card, even after you're out.
  • No tapping, coughing, or giggling at night.
  • What the host says goes.

Two Rooms and a Boom

Two teams, split across two rooms. People move between rooms each round. At the end, if the Bomber is in the same room as the King, Red wins. If not, Blue wins.

The host gives you a chit with a number on it. Keep it hidden.

Before you start

  • The host splits everyone into two rooms: the living room and the den.
  • Each room points at someone to be its leader. If most of the room points at someone else later, that person takes over.

Three rounds, each one shorter

3 minthen swap 2
2 minthen swap 1
1 minthen swap 1
  1. Talk. Go up to anyone and quietly show them your chit. Show just the number, or the whole thing. You can say whatever you want, but the chit doesn't lie. You can also say no.
  2. Choose who goes. When time's up, each leader picks who to send to the other room. Leaders can't send themselves.
  3. Swap. Those people switch rooms at the same time. No talking across rooms.

How to win

After the last swap, everyone shows their chit.

  • Red wins if the Bomber and the King are in the same room.
  • Blue wins if they're not.

Tips

  • King: only show your number until you're sure who you're talking to.
  • Bomber: act like a friendly Blue. Ask people to show you first.
  • The leader decides who moves. If you have a plan, try to become leader.
  • Keep an eye on whoever won't show their chit.
